How the builder works

Every leg's chance comes from the same models as the sport pages, anchored to Pinnacle's price. A ticket's real chance is all its legs' chances multiplied together, one leg per game, because legs from the same game move together.

For the odds you choose, the builder picks the combination with the highest real chance. Usually that means fewer legs: each extra leg adds the bookmaker's margin again.

Reading the ticket

Fair is each leg's break-even price. If your bookmaker pays less than fair on a leg, swap it for another or leave it out.

Real chance is how often a ticket like this lands. A 5-leg ticket at 10.00 landing 9% of the time is about 1 in 11.

Legs you add stay on the ticket when you build again, and the builder fills the rest around them.

Before you bet

Accumulators are the bookmaker's favourite bet: the margin compounds on every leg. Small stakes only, never more than you can afford to lose. 18+.

Odds are a snapshot and move quickly. Check every leg's live price before placing anything.
