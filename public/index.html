How the picks are made

Bookmaker prices for the result, goal lines and both-teams-to-score are stripped of their margin, then the engine finds the goal rates for each team that best reproduce them. Those rates are blended 75/25 with a form rating from each side's last 20 games (goals and xG for and against) and this competition's games.

A Dixon-Coles score grid turns the two goal rates into every scoreline, and from there into results, handicaps, totals and BTTS. Corners and cards use over-dispersed count models fed by team averages and the referee's record. Scorer and assist chances share out each team's expected goals by recent shots, goals and assists, scaled by how likely the player is to start.

Reading the numbers

Fair odds are what the model thinks a bet is worth with no bookmaker margin. Only take a price above it.

Edge compares the model's chance with the odds on offer: Strong (clear edge), Lean (small edge), Fair price (about even) or Thin (the bookmaker's price is short).

Lineups say Predicted until the official team sheets are out, about an hour before kick-off. Then they switch to Confirmed and the numbers are re-run.

Before you bet

These are probabilities, not certainties. A 60% pick still loses four times in ten, and with odds this close to the market most bets carry no real edge. Never stake more than you can afford to lose. 18+ only.

Odds are a snapshot from the time shown at the top and move quickly. Check the live price before placing anything.
