How the picks are made

Bookmaker prices for the result, goal lines and both-teams-to-score are stripped of their margin, then the engine finds the goal rates for each team that best reproduce them. Those rates are blended 75/25 with each side's scoring record in the competition this season, and trimmed a little for heavy rain, strong wind, heat or snow at the venue.

A Dixon-Coles score grid turns the two goal rates into every scoreline, and from there into results, handicaps, totals and BTTS. Corners and cards follow the bookmaker's line when one is offered, otherwise team averages. Scorer and assist chances share out each team's expected goals by the players' goals, assists and shots, scaled by how likely each one is to start.

Where the data comes from

Fixtures, odds, referees, injuries, suspensions, confirmed lineups and top scorers: API-Football. Weather at the venue: Open-Meteo. Team news headlines: Google News.

Lineups say Predicted (the team's last starting XI minus injured and suspended players) until the official team sheets arrive, about an hour before kick-off. Then they switch to Confirmed and every number is re-run. The page refreshes itself every five minutes.

Before you bet

These are probabilities, not certainties. A 60% pick still loses four times in ten, and with odds this close to the market most bets carry no real edge. Never stake more than you can afford to lose. 18+ only.

Fair odds are what the model thinks a bet is worth. Only take a price above it.
